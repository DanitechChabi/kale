## Ce que tu vas apprendre
- Nommer les pièces d'une maison et quelques meubles.
- Utiliser **there is** et **there are** pour dire ce qu'il y a dans un lieu.
- Situer les objets avec des prépositions de lieu (*in, on, under, next to*).

## THERE IS / THERE ARE : il y a

Pour dire « il y a », l'anglais utilise **there is** ou **there are**.

| Forme | Utilisation | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| **there is** (contraction : *there's*) | devant un nom **singulier** | *There is a kitchen.* (Il y a une cuisine.) |
| **there are** | devant un nom **pluriel** | *There are three bedrooms.* (Il y a trois chambres.) |

> **Règle** : Regarde le nom qui suit : un seul objet → *there is* ; plusieurs objets → *there are*.

> **Règle** : Avec *there is*, on met **a** ou **an** devant le nom : *There is a fan. There is an armchair.* On utilise **an** devant un son voyelle (a, e, i, o, u).

### La forme négative

On ajoute **not** :

- *There isn't a garden.* (Il n'y a pas de jardin.)
- *There aren't any windows in the toilet.* (Il n'y a pas de fenêtres dans les toilettes.)

> **Règle** : Au pluriel, à la forme négative, on ajoute **any** devant le nom : *There aren't any chairs.*

### La forme interrogative

On inverse : le verbe passe devant *there*.

| Question | Oui | Non |
|---|---|---|
| Is there a TV? | Yes, there is. | No, there isn't. |
| Are there any chairs? | Yes, there are. | No, there aren't. |
| How many rooms are there? | There are five rooms. | — |

> **Règle** : *How many…?* (combien de) est toujours suivi d'un nom au pluriel et de *are there* : *How many bedrooms are there?*

## Les prépositions de lieu

| English | Français | Exemple |
|---|---|---|
| in | dans | *The bed is in the bedroom.* |
| on | sur | *The TV is on the table.* |
| under | sous | *The cat is under the chair.* |
| next to | à côté de | *The kitchen is next to the bathroom.* |
| behind | derrière | *The garden is behind the house.* |
| in front of | devant | *The car is in front of the door.* |
| between | entre | *The sitting room is between the kitchen and the bedroom.* |

## Dialogue — Bio visite la nouvelle maison de Koffi à Abomey-Calavi

> **Bio** : Your new house is beautiful, Koffi!
> **Koffi** : Thank you. Come in! This is the sitting room.
> **Bio** : There's a big TV! Is there a fan?
> **Koffi** : Yes, there is. It's on the wall, next to the window.
> **Bio** : How many bedrooms are there?
> **Koffi** : There are three bedrooms: my parents' room, my sisters' room and my room.
> **Bio** : Is there a garden?
> **Koffi** : No, there isn't, but there's a big yard behind the house. There are two mango trees in the yard.
> **Bio** : Great! Where is the kitchen?
> **Koffi** : It's next to the dining room. Come, my mother is cooking!

Vocabulaire : *beautiful* (« biou-ti-feul ») = belle ; *come in* = entre ; *mango tree* = manguier ; *cooking* = en train de cuisiner.

## Texte modèle : My house

> *I live in a house in Parakou with my family. It isn't very big, but it's nice. There are five rooms: a sitting room, two bedrooms, a kitchen and a bathroom. In the sitting room, there are four armchairs, a table and a TV. My bedroom is small. There is a bed, a chair and a cupboard. There isn't a garden, but there is a yard with a well. My grandmother sits on a mat in the yard in the evening.*

Vocabulaire : *nice* = agréable ; *well* (« ouèl ») = puits ; *sits* = s'assoit.

## À retenir
- Pièces : *sitting room / living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, toilet, dining room, yard*.
- *There is* + nom singulier (avec *a* / *an*) ; *there are* + nom pluriel.
- Négation : *there isn't a… / there aren't any…*
- Question : *Is there a…? / Are there any…? / How many… are there?*
- Prépositions : *in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between*.

=== EXERCICES ===
### Exercice 1 — facile
Dans quelle pièce fait-on ces actions ? Réponds en anglais.

1. On dort.  
2. On prépare le repas.  
3. On regarde la télévision avec la famille.  
4. On se lave.

#### Corrigé
1. **the bedroom**  
2. **the kitchen**  
3. **the sitting room** (ou *the living room*)  
4. **the bathroom**

### Exercice 2 — facile
Complète avec **there is** ou **there are**.

1. ___ a bed in my room.  
2. ___ four chairs in the kitchen.  
3. ___ an armchair in the sitting room.  
4. ___ two windows in the bedroom.

#### Corrigé
1. **There is** a bed (singulier).  
2. **There are** four chairs (pluriel).  
3. **There is** an armchair (singulier ; *an* devant une voyelle).  
4. **There are** two windows (pluriel).

### Exercice 3 — moyen
Mets à la forme négative.

1. There is a fridge in the kitchen.  
2. There are chairs in the yard.  
3. There is a TV in my bedroom.

#### Corrigé
1. There **isn't** a fridge in the kitchen.  
2. There **aren't any** chairs in the yard. (au pluriel, on ajoute *any*)  
3. There **isn't** a TV in my bedroom.

### Exercice 4 — moyen
Pose la question qui correspond à chaque réponse.

1. Yes, there is. There's a fan in the sitting room.  
2. No, there aren't. There aren't any trees in the yard.  
3. There are four bedrooms.

#### Corrigé
1. **Is there a fan in the sitting room?**  
2. **Are there any trees in the yard?**  
3. **How many bedrooms are there?**

### Exercice 5 — approfondissement
Traduis en anglais.

1. Le chat est sous la table.  
2. La cuisine est à côté de la salle de bains.  
3. Il y a un manguier derrière la maison.  
4. Combien de fenêtres y a-t-il dans le salon ?

#### Corrigé
1. **The cat is under the table.**  
2. **The kitchen is next to the bathroom.**  
3. **There is a mango tree behind the house.**  
4. **How many windows are there in the sitting room?** (ou *living room*)
